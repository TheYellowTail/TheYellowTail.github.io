Wintermaul Now You Lose Details - Difficult Levels and Their Mechanics:

Early Game (Basic tower Defense)

Level 13 Unstoppable Abominations:

Players are certain to leak on this level. This level tests how well you can manage the basics of the game.

Mid Game (Strategic Preparation)

Level 16 Nightcrawlers:

The first boss new players usually lose on this level. This level can decimate players, or it can be held spot on by the last defender. The last defender is required to prepare for this level.

Level 18 Unstoppable Blood Feasters:

Tests your knowledge of semi-ultimate towers and makes your transition into ultimate towers difficult, as this level punishes early ultimate tower transition.

Level 23 Evasion Air:

This level requires strong anti air preparation. To hold this level effectively you either need the Air race or use the ultimate tower from Heaven race Heavens Might. Even with preparation you can still lose with the enemy 50% evasion on attacks.

Level 25 Mantorok The Dead God:

This level requires at least one player on the team to build the very expensive Laser Cannon tower. You will most likely lose without a Laser Cannon because Mantorok regenerates a lot of hit points nullifying weak towers.

Level 26 Unstoppable Dread Lord Champions:

This level is like level 13 where you are certain to leak and lose lives. You must spend your resources. Having your ultimate tower upgrade and an ultimate tower is required for this level.

Level 28 Dragoth The Drake Lord:

You must continue to spend your resources instead of saving up or you will for sure lose on this level.

Late Game (Survival)

The late game usually requires armor type countering with different tower attack types to succeed.

Level 35 Vengeful Champions: Fortified Armor

These enemies spawn in groups of 10 instead of 20 and regenerate hit points. You will be punished for making too many splash towers. There are some specific ultimate towers that are effective against this level. To beat this level effectively you must have one of these specific ultimate towers.

Level 36 Unstoppable Hellfire Infernals:

These enemies have a lot of hit points but no armor which makes spell damage ultimate towers a lot less effective. This level is also a Medium Armor type level which requires strong preparation to counter this armor type. You will lose lives even if you prepare perfectly. You must decide to prepare heavily to lose less lives, prepare moderately, or don’t prepare at all and leak the whole level but be able to prepare for future levels.

Level 39 Twilight Minister: Fortified Armor

This boss requires each player to have at least 2 laser cannons.

Level 40 Air:

This air level is very powerful and requires players to invest heavily in anti air at the same time as preparing for the previous levels.

Level 41-43:

You will need a variety of armor countering and make use of ultimate tower splash and area of effect damage for these levels.
